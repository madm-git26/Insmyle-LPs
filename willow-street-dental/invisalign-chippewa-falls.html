Invisalign and Clear Aligners in Chippewa Falls, WI

If you've been searching for Invisalign in Chippewa Falls, you're probably looking for a more discreet way to work on your smile than traditional metal braces. Clear aligners are transparent, custom-made trays that fit closely over your teeth and are designed to gradually guide them into a straighter position over time.

Many adults and teens consider clear aligners because they want an option that fits into daily life without drawing a lot of attention — whether that's at work, at school, or in photos. Every smile is different, though, and clear aligners aren't automatically the right fit for every situation.

At Willow Street Dental, Dr. Curtis Calder, D.D.S. and our Chippewa Falls team can help you understand your options, starting with a conversation about your smile goals. Because clear aligner candidacy depends on your individual teeth, bite, and oral health, a consultation — virtual or in office — is the appropriate first step before any treatment decisions are made.

Benefits

Why Consider Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners offer a different approach to straightening teeth than traditional braces. Here's what patients often appreciate about them.

A More Discreet Way to Straighten Teeth

Clear aligners are designed to be virtually invisible when worn, so they tend to blend in with your natural smile. Many patients like that they can work toward their smile goals without the more noticeable look of metal brackets and wires.

Comfortable, Custom-Fitted Trays

Because each set of aligners is custom-made for your teeth, they're designed to fit closely and comfortably. As with any new dental appliance, it can take a little time to adjust to wearing a new set.

Removable for Eating and Drinking

Clear aligners can be taken out, so you're not restricted from the foods you enjoy the way you might be with fixed braces. Most patients remove them to eat and to drink anything other than water.

Easier Oral Hygiene

Because clear aligners come out, brushing and flossing can be more straightforward than navigating around brackets and wires. Removing your aligners for regular oral care can help you keep up with your normal routine.

A Personalized Treatment Plan

Your clear aligner plan is built around your own teeth and smile goals. Digital scans and imaging are used to map out how your teeth may move over the course of treatment, so your plan is designed specifically for you.

A Convenient Path to Exploring Your Options

You don't have to commit to anything to start learning more. A consultation — including a free virtual consultation — gives you a low-pressure way to ask questions and find out whether clear aligners may be a good fit for your smile.

Is It Right For You?

Could Clear Aligners Be Right for You?

Clear aligners may help address a range of mild to moderate concerns, depending on your evaluation, including:

  • Crowded teeth
  • Spacing between teeth
  • Certain bite concerns, such as overbites, underbites, or crossbites
  • Minor tooth rotations

Good to Know

What Is the Difference Between Invisalign and Clear Aligners?

“Invisalign” is a well-known brand name within the broader category of clear aligner treatment, which is why many patients search for “Invisalign” even when they're really looking for clear, removable aligners in general. “Clear aligners” is the broader term for this type of treatment.

Willow Street Dental's Chippewa Falls clear aligner service is described on our site as clear aligner treatment. If you're specifically interested in a particular clear aligner brand, including Invisalign, the best way to find out what may be available for your smile is to ask our team during your consultation.
