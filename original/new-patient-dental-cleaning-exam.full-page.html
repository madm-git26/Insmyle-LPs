Your First Visit

What to Expect as a New Patient at In Smyle Dental

We've designed your first visit to be welcoming, informative, and completely pressure-free. Here's exactly what happens when you walk through our door.

1

Warm Welcome & Health History Review

Our friendly front-desk team greets you and walks through your dental and medical history in detail, so your doctor can provide safe, personalized care from the very first moment.

2

Comprehensive Oral Examination

Your dentist performs a thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw, identifying any concerns early before they become costly procedures. Digital X-rays are taken as needed.

3

Professional Dental Cleaning

Our hygienists carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and give you a personalized oral hygiene plan to maintain your results between visits.

4

Your Custom Treatment Plan

We present any recommended next steps with complete transparency — explaining costs, insurance coverage, and financing options. You decide what works for you, on your timeline.

Common things new patients ask before their first dental cleaning and exam in Chicago.

Please bring a valid photo ID, your dental insurance card (if applicable), and a list of any current medications. Arriving 10 minutes early helps us complete your intake paperwork before your appointment time. If you've had dental X-rays taken recently at another office, you're welcome to bring those or request a transfer — though we typically take our own updated digital X-rays for a thorough baseline evaluation.
Your first comprehensive visit — which includes the health history review, full-mouth digital X-rays, dental exam, and professional cleaning — typically takes between 60 and 90 minutes. We never rush you. This thorough approach allows us to catch issues early and start building a long-term relationship based on your individual oral health goals.
Not at all. We welcome patients with or without dental insurance. Most dental professionals — including our team — recommend two professional cleanings and exams per year. However, patients with gum disease, a history of cavities, or other risk factors may benefit from more frequent visits. During your first appointment, your dentist will recommend the ideal schedule based on your specific oral health needs.
A professional dental cleaning is generally comfortable for most patients. If you have sensitive teeth, gum sensitivity, or dental anxiety, please let us know before we begin. Our hygienists use a gentle technique and can adjust their approach to keep you at ease throughout the entire visit.
