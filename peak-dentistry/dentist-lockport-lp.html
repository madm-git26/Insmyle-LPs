Your first visit

What actually happens

No mystery, no upsell ambush. Four steps, start to finish.

01

You get greeted, not processed

Front desk checks you in, confirms your details, and goes over insurance and payment options before you sit down. Nervous? Say so — it changes how we work.

02

Pick your comfort options

Cozy blankets and pillows, wireless headphones, in-room TVs, warm towels, and a beverage bar in the lobby. Take whatever makes the chair easier.

03

A thorough, gentle exam

Digital X-rays, a gum and tooth health check, an oral cancer screening, and a cleaning in most cases. Around 45 minutes to an hour, and we explain each step as we go.

04

A plan with the costs on it

You sit with Dr. Djordjevic, review what he found, and get options rather than pressure. Then the treatment coordinator covers insurance, out-of-pocket and financing.

What's in the room

The tech doing the work

Fewer visits, fewer impressions, better answers about what's actually going on.

CEREC

Same-day crowns

Your crown is scanned, designed on screen and milled in the office. You leave with the final ceramic restoration, not a temporary.

CBCT

3D imaging

A cone beam scan maps bone, nerves and sinuses in three dimensions — which is how implant placement gets planned safely instead of estimated.

Scanner

Digital impressions

A small intraoral scanner replaces impression trays for crowns, aligners, night guards and surgical guides. Nothing sticky, nothing to gag on.

3D print

In-office lab work

Models and surgical guides are printed here from your scan, which keeps the fit tight and the turnaround short.

VELscope

Oral cancer screening

Enhanced screening at your checkup, looking for tissue changes that aren't obvious to the naked eye.

DiagnoDent

Early cavity detection

Finds decay earlier, which usually means a smaller, more conservative fix instead of a bigger one later.
