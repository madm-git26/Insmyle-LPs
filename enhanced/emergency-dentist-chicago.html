In Dental Pain? Here's Exactly What to Do in the Next 5 Minutes

Follow these steps while you arrange your urgent dental care appointment. They help protect the tooth and reduce pain before you reach us.

  • 1
    Call us first — dial (773) 915-6530. Describing your symptoms lets us prepare the right treatment room before you arrive.
  • 2
    Bleeding? Bite down gently on clean gauze or a damp cloth for 10–15 minutes without checking it repeatedly.
  • 3
    Swelling or trauma? Hold a cold compress against the outside of the cheek for 15 minutes at a time.
  • 4
    Knocked-out tooth? Handle it by the crown only — never the root. Rinse gently with water and keep it moist in milk, or between your cheek and gum. Time is critical.
  • 5
    Lost a crown or filling? Keep the piece and bring it with you — it can often be re-cemented the same day.
  • 6
    Never place aspirin directly on the gum — it can burn the soft tissue and make treatment harder.
  • 7
    Rinse with warm salt water and avoid very hot, very cold, hard or sugary food until you've been seen.
