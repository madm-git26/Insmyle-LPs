What happens when you come in

From the phone call to walking back out — and in most cases the problem is dealt with in that one visit.

  • 1

    Call us or just turn up

    Tell us what's going on and we'll triage it over the phone, then put you in the first urgent slot — often within a couple of hours.

  • 2

    A focused exam and X-ray

    We're looking for the source of the pain, not doing a full check-up. Digital imaging finds it quickly.

  • 3

    Getting you comfortable

    Pain relief comes before any conversation about treatment plans or costs. That part can wait ten minutes.

  • 4

    Treated the same visit

    Most emergencies are finished there and then — a filling, root canal treatment, an extraction, or a same-day crown.

Questions we get asked a lot

The things people ask us on the phone when they are in pain and need answers fast.

Can you really see me today?
In most cases, yes. We hold urgent slots back every day we are open, so if you are searching for an emergency dentist near me right now, call (773) 915-6530. Ringing in the morning usually means being seen that afternoon.
Do you take walk-ins?
Yes. We are a walk-in dentist in Chicago — if you are in too much pain to sit on hold, just come in. Calling ahead helps us have a chair ready for you, but it is not essential.
Is there an urgent care dental clinic near me that is open now?
That is us, within our opening hours: Mondays and Thursdays until 7:00 pm, Tuesdays and Wednesdays until 6:00 pm, and Saturdays 7:00 am to 3:00 pm, starting at 7:00 am most mornings. We are closed Fridays and Sundays.
What counts as a dental emergency?
Severe toothache or nerve pain, a chipped or cracked tooth, a filling or crown that has come out, a knocked-out tooth, facial or gum swelling, bleeding that will not settle, an infection, or a tooth that needs taking out.
Should I go to the emergency room instead?
For facial trauma, bleeding you cannot stop, or trouble breathing or swallowing, go straight to the nearest hospital ER or call 911. For everything else on the list above, an emergency dentist in Chicago is faster, cheaper and far more likely to actually fix the problem — a hospital will usually only give you painkillers and send you to a dentist anyway.
My tooth has been knocked out — what do I do right now?
Pick it up by the crown, never the root. Rinse it gently if it is dirty, do not scrub it, and keep it moist in milk. Then call us immediately — the sooner we see you, the better the chance of saving it.
Are you an emergency dentist open Saturday and in the evenings?
Yes. Saturdays 7:00 am to 3:00 pm, and Mondays and Thursdays until 7:00 pm for anything that flares up after work. Tuesdays and Wednesdays we are here until 6:00 pm. Closed Fridays and Sundays.
Will it be sorted in one visit?
Most emergencies are. Fillings, root canal treatment, extractions and same-day crowns are all done here, so you usually leave with the problem treated rather than booked in for later.
Do I need to be an existing patient, or have a referral?
Neither. Most of the people we see in an emergency have never been here before, and no referral is needed.
What will it cost if I do not have insurance?
You will get the number before we start — we do not begin treatment and hand you a surprise afterwards. Our membership plan includes an emergency exam and takes 15% off treatment, and Cherry, Sunbit and CareCredit let you spread the cost over monthly payments.
Do you take my dental insurance?
We are in network with most major carriers, including Delta Dental, Aetna, Cigna, MetLife, Humana, Guardian, Anthem and United Healthcare. Give us your insurer on the phone and we will have your benefits confirmed by the time you arrive, then file the claim for you.
Where are you, and how quickly can I get there?
3514 N Lincoln Ave, Chicago, IL 60657 — an emergency dentist in Lakeview, on the Roscoe Village side. A short walk from the CTA Brown Line at Paulina or Southport, minutes from Belmont and Addison, with street parking around the block.
