Your emergency visit, step by step

Here's exactly what happens when you come in with dental pain — and in most cases you leave the same day with the problem treated.

  • 1

    Call us or walk in

    Tell us what's happening. We triage over the phone and get you into the first available urgent slot — often within hours.

  • 2

    Exam and digital X-rays

    We find the source of the pain quickly with a focused exam and digital imaging — no guesswork, no delay.

  • 3

    Pain relief comes first

    We get you comfortable and out of pain before anything else, then explain your options and costs clearly.

  • 4

    Same-day treatment

    Most emergencies are handled in the same visit — fillings, root canal treatment, extractions or a same-day crown.

📋 What to do right now

  • ✓ Knocked-out tooth: hold it by the crown only, rinse gently, keep it moist in milk and call us immediately
  • ✓ Swelling or bleeding: call us straight away — don't wait for it to settle on its own
  • ✓ Bring a list of the medications you take regularly
  • ✓ Bring your dental insurance details — we'll verify your coverage before treatment

⚠️ Important: for facial trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital ER or call 911.
