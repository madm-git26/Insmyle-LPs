Same-day dentistry

A crown finished before Saturday lunch.

Our in-house scanner and milling unit replace three weeks of waiting, a temporary crown and a second round of numbing with one appointment — usually around two hours start to finish.

1

Digital scan

A wand-sized camera photographs the prepared tooth in 3D. No impression trays, no gagging, no goo.

2

Design with you

The crown is designed on screen in front of you. Shape, bite and contact points adjusted before anything is made.

3

Milled on site

A solid block of ceramic is milled to your design in the room next door — usually in twelve to fifteen minutes.

4

Fitted and polished

We check the bite, glaze, polish and cement. You leave with the finished tooth, not a placeholder.

Questions

The things people ask before booking.

When are you open?
Friday 12:00pm to 7:00pm, Saturday 10:00am to 3:00pm and Sunday 10:00am to 3:00pm. Those hours are deliberate — most people should not have to burn a vacation day to get a filling. Call or email any time and we will reply on the next clinic day.
How much is a new patient visit?
Our new patient visit is $55 and covers a comprehensive exam with Dr. Khan, a full set of low-dose digital X-rays and intraoral photographs, plus a written treatment plan with your insurance applied. Anything you go on to have done is quoted in writing first — you decide in your own time.
Do you take my insurance?
We are in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, Guardian, United Concordia, United Healthcare, Humana and GEHA Connection — and we happily file out-of-network claims for everyone else. Bring your card to the first visit and our treatment coordinator will verify your benefits before any work is scheduled.
What if I do not have dental insurance?
The $55 new patient visit is the same price either way, so you can see exactly where you stand before committing to anything. For treatment after that, CareCredit and Sunbit offer 6, 12 and 24-month plans, several of them interest-free, with approval decisions in the office in a few minutes.
Can you really do a crown in one appointment?
Yes. We scan the prepared tooth digitally, design the crown on screen, mill it from a solid ceramic block in our lab room and cement it the same visit — usually around two hours from start to finish.
I have not been to a dentist in years. Will I get a lecture?
No. Roughly a third of our new patients have been away for five years or more. We start where you are, deal with anything urgent first, then build a phased plan you actually feel comfortable saying yes to.
How long will the first appointment take?
Long enough to do it properly — the exam, a full set of X-rays and intraoral photographs, plus time at the end to go through every finding on screen with you. We will give you the exact length when we book you in.
Do you see children?
We do — from the first tooth onwards. Families routinely book back-to-back appointments so one trip covers everybody, and first visits for little ones are deliberately short and low-key.
What if I have a dental emergency?
We hold emergency slots open on every clinic day, all year. Call (817) 839-7412 with the details — broken teeth, lost crowns, swelling and pain that will not wait are what those slots exist for.
