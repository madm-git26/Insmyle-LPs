Questions

Emergency dentistry, answered.

Can I be seen today?
Call before 2 pm on a clinic day (Friday, Saturday or Sunday) and we will do everything we can to see you the same day. We keep protected slots open every morning specifically for emergencies. Call (817) 839-7412.
What if my emergency happens Monday to Thursday?
The office is open Friday 12–7 pm and Saturday and Sunday 10 am–3 pm. Outside those hours, call and leave a message: Dr. Khan provides after-hours phone triage and will tell you how to manage until the next clinic day.
Will it hurt?
Modern anaesthesia, topical gel before every injection and unhurried appointments mean the overwhelming majority of our patients describe treatment as far easier than they expected. If you are anxious, tell us when you book and we schedule extra time for it.
Does insurance cover emergency dentistry?
Coverage varies by plan and by procedure code. We verify your benefits before treatment is scheduled and give you a written estimate showing what your carrier pays and what you pay. Members of our in-house plan receive 15–20% off emergency dentistry. No insurance? CareCredit and Sunbit offer 6 to 24-month plans, several interest-free.
Can you fix a broken tooth in one visit?
Very often, yes. We scan the tooth digitally, design the crown on screen, mill it from a solid ceramic block in our lab room and cement it the same visit, usually around two hours from start to finish. No temporary crown and no second trip.
