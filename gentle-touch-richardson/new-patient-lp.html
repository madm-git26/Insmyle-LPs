10New Patient FAQs

Questions before your first visit?

Are you accepting new patients in Richardson?
Yes. Our Richardson dental office welcomes new patients of all ages, from toddlers to seniors, and we offer same-day appointments for dental emergencies whenever possible. You can book online in a few clicks or call (469) 336-3069.
What should I expect at my first visit?
Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital x-rays, and intraoral photos that Dr. Chepyala reviews with you on a large 40-inch screen. We perform a thorough gum exam, explain your options in plain language, and when time allows, complete your cleaning the same day.
How long does the first appointment take?
A routine cleaning and exam usually takes about an hour. Your first visit may take a little longer because we take a complete set of digital x-rays and review your health history.
What should I bring?
Bring your insurance card, and complete your patient forms before you arrive to save time.
What dental insurance do you accept?
We are in-network with 29+ PPO dental plans, including Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental, MetLife, United Healthcare, Guardian, GEHA, Humana, BCBS, Anthem, Ameritas, Principal, UMR, and United Concordia. If your plan isn't listed, call us — we may still be able to accept it. We do not accept Medicaid, HMO or state insurance plans.
What if I don't have dental insurance?
Our in-office membership plan makes dental care affordable for you and your family, with immediate benefits and 12 months of coverage starting the day you enroll. Financing is also available for implants and other major care. Ask our team for details.
I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?
Yes. Comfort is at the heart of everything we do. We take our time, explain each step, and offer comforts like blankets, pillows, headphones, and TVs in our treatment rooms. Nitrous oxide and oral sedation are available to help anxious patients relax.
Do you see children?
Yes. Dr. Chepyala is specially trained in pediatric dentistry, and we follow the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommendation of a first visit by your child's first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth appearing. For anxious kids, Dr. Chepyala can create a personalized sedation plan.
Can my whole family be seen on the same day?
In many cases, yes. We try to coordinate back-to-back appointments for family members so you can make one trip instead of several. Let our team know when you call or book how many people you would like to schedule.
Do you offer same-day emergency appointments?
Yes, whenever possible. Please call (469) 336-3069 first so our team can review your symptoms, give you first-aid advice, and reserve the soonest opening. For life-threatening emergencies, call 911.
Where are you located, and what are your hours?
We are at 1750 Alma Rd #110, Richardson, TX 75081, near Prairie Creek Park — a short drive from Plano and Garland. We are open Monday 8 am–5 pm, Wednesday 10 am–6:30 pm, Thursday 10 am–4 pm, Friday 7 am–4:30 pm, and Saturday 10 am–2 pm by appointment only. We are closed Tuesday and Sunday.
