Emergencies we treat

Urgent Dental Care for Every Kind of Emergency

Dental emergencies can be scary and painful. Our McMurray team is here to help you feel calm and confident while you get the quick, effective treatment you need.

  • Severe toothache

    Throbbing pain or sudden sensitivity. We find the cause and get you comfortable.

  • Broken, chipped or cracked tooth

    Restored with a white filling or an all-porcelain crown, depending on the damage.

  • Knocked-out tooth

    Time matters. Keep it in milk and come straight in — it may be possible to save it.

  • Loose or dislodged tooth

    Avoid biting on it. We’ll check it with digital imaging and explain your options.

  • Swelling or abscess

    A gum “pimple”, swelling or bad taste can mean infection that needs prompt care.

  • Lost crown or filling

    Broken crowns and fillings are emergencies we treat. Bring the crown with you.

  • Wisdom tooth pain

    Swollen, painful wisdom teeth? We offer wisdom teeth removal right here in McMurray.

  • Bleeding gums

    Bleeding that won’t settle, or sore, swollen gums. Bite on clean gauze and call us.

  • Object stuck between teeth

    Don’t dig at it with anything sharp. Floss gently — if it won’t budge, we’ll help.

  • Mouth, lip or jaw injury

    Trauma to the teeth, gums, lips or jaw needs a prompt exam to check for damage.

Not sure if it’s an emergency?

Call us and tell us what’s going on — we’ll let you know what to do next.

Your Emergency Visit, Step by Step

No guesswork and no pressure — just fast answers, real relief and a clear plan.

  1. Call or book online

    Call (724) 941-2200 and tell us what happened — we’ll find you the soonest opening. You can also book online 24/7.

Emergency Dental Care FAQs

Do you offer same-day emergency dental appointments?

Yes. Omni Dental McMurray offers same-day emergency dental appointments, and we make every effort to see you as soon as possible. Calling (724) 941-2200 is the fastest way to get the next available opening — you can also book online 24/7.

What if my emergency happens after hours or on a weekend?

Don’t hesitate to call our office at any time. Our after-hours message gives Dr. Hanlon’s personal cell phone number so you can reach him directly. If you have facial swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing, bleeding that won’t stop, or a head or jaw injury, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Do you see new patients for dental emergencies?

Absolutely. New patients and emergency appointments are welcome. You don’t need to be an existing patient to be seen for an emergency.

Can I just walk in?

Please call (724) 941-2200 before you head over. Calling first lets our team find the soonest opening for you and be ready when you arrive.
