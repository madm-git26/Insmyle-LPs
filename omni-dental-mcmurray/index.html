FAQ

Questions From New Patients

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! New patients and emergency appointments are welcome at our McMurray office. You can book online anytime, or call (724) 220-6862.

Can I get a same-day or emergency appointment?

Yes. We offer same-day appointments and after-hours emergency care. If you have a toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a broken crown or filling, an abscess or swelling, call us right away. We understand there's no pain like tooth pain and make every effort to see you as soon as possible.

Which dental insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most PPO plans, including Delta Dental, Aetna PPO, Cigna PPO, MetLife, United Concordia, Guardian, UPMC Dental Advantage PPO and UPMC for Life (Medicare Advantage). Call us to verify your plan. If you share your insurance details before your visit, we'll check your benefits so you know what to expect.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

No problem. Our in-office membership plan takes less than 15 minutes to join, and your coverage is active immediately. We also offer financing through CareCredit and Cherry, and accept cash, checks and credit cards.

I'm nervous about going to the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Let us know you're apprehensive and we'll adapt your treatment around your fears. You're always in control, and treatment starts and stops when you say so. Our massaging heated chairs, weighted blankets, headphones and Netflix help you relax. We also offer oral sedation, and for severe anxiety we can arrange in-office sedation with Ambulatory Anesthesia.

What happens during a dental cleaning?

Your hygienist first examines your gums for signs of recession or periodontal pocketing, then carefully removes plaque and tartar around the gum line and between your teeth. Next, they polish your teeth with a high-tech electric brush and finish by flossing. Each visit also includes an oral cancer screening.

Does a dental cleaning hurt?

If it's been a while since your last cleaning, you may notice some gum bleeding. Our hygienists are very gentle and work at a pace that keeps you comfortable, and you can stop or pause at any time.

How often should I have a cleaning and checkup?

We recommend visiting at least twice a year. Routine cleanings remove stubborn plaque and tartar that can lead to tooth decay and gum disease, and your checkup lets us spot cavities, gum disease and other problems early. Dental insurance usually covers twice-yearly cleanings.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes. Our digital X-rays expose you to a very low amount of radiation, which poses a much smaller risk than an undetected dental problem. Our 3D cone beam CT also uses a much lower dose than a conventional medical CT scan.

Do you see children?

Yes, we care for patients of all ages. We recommend children be seen by about age three, so their first visits are positive ones before any dental problem appears.
