FAQ

Questions From New Patients

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! New patients and emergency appointments are welcome at our McMurray office. You can book online anytime, or call (724) 220-6862.

Can I get a same-day or emergency appointment?

Yes. We offer same-day appointments and after-hours emergency care. If you have a toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a broken crown or filling, an abscess or swelling, call us right away. We understand there's no pain like tooth pain and make every effort to see you as soon as possible.

Which dental insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most PPO plans, including Delta Dental, Aetna PPO, Cigna PPO, MetLife, United Concordia, Guardian, UPMC Dental Advantage PPO and UPMC for Life (Medicare Advantage). Call us to verify your plan. If you share your insurance details before your visit, we'll check your benefits so you know what to expect.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

No problem. Our in-office membership plan takes less than 15 minutes to join, and your coverage is active immediately. We also offer financing through CareCredit and Cherry, and accept cash, checks and credit cards.
