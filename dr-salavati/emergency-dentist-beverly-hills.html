Why this practice, for this problem

A broken tooth is not a patch job

Prosthodontics is the dental specialty concerned with rebuilding and replacing teeth — crowns, bridges, implants, dentures, full-mouth reconstruction. Dr. Salavati is board-certified in it, with a three-year residency in implant and prosthodontic surgery on top of dental school. That matters more than it sounds when the thing that has gone wrong is a tooth in pieces.

  • A crown that keeps coming off is a diagnosis, not an accident. Re-cementing it without working out why it failed just books you a second emergency.
  • Broken teeth get assessed for what's salvageable — how much healthy structure is left decides whether you're looking at a filling, a crown, or something more involved. You'll be told honestly which.
  • Front teeth are treated as front teeth. If the break is somewhere visible, matching shape and shade is part of the job, not an upsell afterwards.
  • The emergency fix is planned around the permanent one. What we do today is chosen so it doesn't limit your options next month.

What an emergency visit costs

The honest answer is that it depends on what's wrong — anyone quoting a flat price before looking at the tooth is guessing. What can be said up front is how payment works, so it is not a second problem on top of the first.

Tell us your plan when you call. The claim is submitted on your behalf, so you are not chasing paperwork after the fact.

CareCredit financing

For patients without coverage, or facing a larger restoration, CareCredit is accepted — alongside major credit cards and personal checks.

Getting you out of pain and the tooth stable can be separated from the final restoration. If spreading it out is easier, say so.
